Walk-Forward Optimizer

Grid-search parameters on a training window, validate on an untouched test window. Discards results that don't hold up out-of-sample.

In-sample optimization alone will find a "great" strategy by curve-fitting noise. This tab splits your data into a training period (parameters are tuned here) and a test period (parameters are only evaluated here, never tuned). Only trust rows where test-period Sharpe and profit factor are reasonably close to train.

Risk & Rules

Read this before you ever connect a strategy to real money.

Why backtests lie

• Overfitting — enough parameters, and any curve fits the past. Use the Optimizer's train/test split, not raw in-sample results.
• Survivorship & data-snooping bias — synthetic and even real historical data can flatter a strategy that happens to fit that one sample.
• Slippage & fees compound — a strategy that trades often can be profitable gross and a loser net. Set commission/slippage honestly, then stress them higher.
• Regime change — a strategy tuned on a trending market can bleed out sideways, and vice versa. Test across multiple synthetic seeds and, ideally, multiple real regimes.

Before risking real capital

1. Walk-forward validate — never trust in-sample metrics alone.
2. Forward-test (paper) for weeks, not hours, across different market conditions.
3. Start with a dedicated sub-account and capital you can fully afford to lose.
4. Never disable withdrawal protections; API keys given to any execution bridge should be trading-only, withdrawals disabled.
5. Diversify across a few uncorrelated strategies/timeframes rather than one "optimized" system.
6. Keep a kill switch: a manual way to flatten all positions and pause the bot instantly.
7. Re-validate periodically — a strategy that worked for 6 months can quietly stop working.

No backtest, optimizer, or paper-trading result — including everything in this app — guarantees future profit. Markets are non-stationary and can move against any strategy, including ones with strong historical statistics. Position size accordingly, and treat every number in this tool as a hypothesis to keep testing, not a promise.
